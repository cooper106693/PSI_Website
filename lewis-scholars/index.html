Jonathan D + Mark C. Lewis Foundation Scholars Program

Jonathan D + Mark C. Lewis Foundation Scholars Program

The PSI internship was developed and is offered by the program directors on a voluntary basis. Research faculty volunteer their time and resources to mentor PSI students. As of 2019, the Jonathan D + Mark C. Lewis Foundation has been generously providing summer stipends for selected PSI students completing internships. The Foundation has created an environment where students from different backgrounds can equitably experience the same internship without creating financial hardship for students.

The Jonathan D + Mark C. Lewis Scholars Program supports the training and teaching of high school students with financial needs. Students must complete a set number of hours during their internship to qualify for the stipend. The application period will be announced during the course.
