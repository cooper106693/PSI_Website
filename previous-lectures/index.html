What type of content can I expect from PSI?

We cover diverse lecture topics and take student feedback into consideration to improve the subsequent year. Topics covered include:

  • Navigating Scientific Literature
  • Biology and Genetics
  • Cancer
  • Data Management
  • Biostatistics
  • Cardiovascular Mechanics
  • Neuroscience
  • Pharmacology
  • Epidemiology
  • Ethics of Human Research
  • Ethics of Animal Research
  • Ethics of Artificial Intelligence
  • Emerging Lab Technologies
